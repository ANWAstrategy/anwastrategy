🧘 Core Philosophy: Psychology & Process Over Profit

Mindset First

At ANWA, our foundational belief is that money is merely a byproduct of disciplined execution. Focusing excessively on P&L, dollar amounts, or financial profit creates psychological friction, emotional attachment, and tilt.

The 3 Pillars of ANWA Discipline:
  • Strict Adherence to Rules: Plan the trade, trade the plan without emotional deviation.
  • Process Mastery: Evaluate success based on decision quality, not outcome.
  • Zero Monetary Obsession: Strip the emotional weight of capital away from the charts.
